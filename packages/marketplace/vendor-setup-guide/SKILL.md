# Vendor Setup Guide

## Overview
Use this skill to build a starter vendor sequence that fits the user's business profile. Focus on reporting behavior, approval likelihood, and practical setup steps.

## Workflow

1. Gather constraints:
   - Industry
   - Entity age
   - Personal guarantee tolerance
   - Budget
   - Existing business credit file
2. Rank vendors by usefulness:
   - Reports to major bureaus
   - Low barrier to approval
   - Realistic spend requirement
   - Clear payment cadence
3. Recommend a staged sequence:
   - Starter account
   - Second and third trade line
   - Expansion accounts after reporting begins
4. Provide setup instructions:
   - Application requirements
   - Billing setup
   - First-purchase guidance
   - Payment timing
5. Call out red flags such as non-reporting accounts, hidden fees, or overbuying.
