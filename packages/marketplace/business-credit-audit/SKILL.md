# Business Credit Audit

## Overview
Use this skill to audit the full business credit footprint, not just one bureau. Start with identity consistency, then move through trade lines, payments, inquiries, public records, and missing setup items.

## Workflow

1. Collect the business profile basics:
   - Legal name
   - DBA
   - Address
   - Phone
   - EIN
   - Entity type
   - Bank account
   - Bureaus or reports available
2. Audit the identity layer for mismatches across sources.
3. Audit reporting data:
   - Open trade lines
   - Payment history
   - Utilization or balances
   - Recent inquiries
   - Collections, liens, judgments, or bankruptcies
4. Group findings by severity:
   - Critical errors
   - Material weaknesses
   - Minor cleanup items
5. End with a prioritized remediation checklist and note any missing evidence needed for a deeper review.
